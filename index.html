Why FUNctional?

Kids learn faster when what they are learning is tied to something functional — something they can actually use. And kids are far more likely to keep going when it is fun. So FUNctional became the foundation of how I teach, and it is at the heart of every Lyman Learning game.

Designed by an OT

Created by an occupational therapist, nervous system coach, and homeschooling mom who understands how kids learn and grow.

Many subjects at once

Our games cross subject areas — like geography and social studies woven together with real math such as fractions and percentages.

The “just right challenge”

Many games adjust to a child’s developmental stage, so the challenge is hard enough to grow and easy enough to stay motivated to keep learning.

Growing from mistakes

It is not just about winning or losing. When kids miss something, they study, adapt, and try again — learning that mistakes are part of growing.

A calmer pace

No flashing effects, countdown timers, or rapid-fire rewards. Our games move at a gentler pace, so they are less likely to overstimulate young nervous systems.

Download & play offline

Install any game on your device and play in airplane mode — safer for little ones, and perfect for car rides and travel.
